Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my check-ins stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud account, so they are there on your other devices too. They never reach us: Nodo has no servers, no accounts, and no way to read what you enter.

Will my check-ins come back if I reinstall the app or change phone?

Yes, as long as you are signed in to iCloud. Your history syncs to your own private iCloud, so reinstalling Nodo or setting up a new iPhone brings it back by itself; give it a minute after the first launch.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Nodo’s Settings and tap Restore Purchases; the button is also on the unlock screen. Your App Store account is used to verify the purchase — make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

I was charged but the purchase isn’t unlocked.

Try Restore Purchases first. If that doesn’t work, please contact us with your Apple Account email and the approximate date of purchase and we’ll help resolve it.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and select the purchase to request a refund.

Can I use Nodo on more than one device?

Yes. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and works on any device signed into it, and your check-ins sync between your devices through your own private iCloud.

Why didn’t I get a reminder?

Reminders only arrive on days you haven’t checked in yet. Make sure Notifications is on in Nodo’s Settings and that notifications are allowed for Nodo in your iPhone’s Settings › Notifications.

How do I check in without opening the app?

Nodo works from a Home Screen widget, a Lock Screen widget, Control Center and the Action Button. In Nodo, open Settings › Check in from anywhere for a step-by-step guide to each.

How do I delete my data?

Inside the app you can delete any check-in from its menu. Deleting the app is not enough on its own, because a copy lives in your iCloud — you can remove that from your iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We’d love to hear from you. Send us an email using the button below and we’ll do our best to address it in an upcoming update.
